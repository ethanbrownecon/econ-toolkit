## Other fields

| Field | What it studies | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| **Behavioral and experimental** | How real people depart from textbook rationality: procrastination, present bias, framing | [The Effect](https://theeffectbook.net/) on experiments; Thaler and Sunstein's *Nudge* |
| **Economic history** | Long-run growth, institutions and past crises | The [Maddison Project](https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/) long-run income data |
| **Sports economics** | Labor markets, incentives and behavior, using sports data | The [home advantage project](../projects/home-advantage.md) |
| **Energy and agricultural** | Energy markets, food prices and farm productivity | [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/opendata/) and [USDA ERS](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/) data |

## How to pick a project

1. **Start from a question you care about,** then find data, not the other way round.
2. **Check the data exist** before you commit. The [datasets page](../data/datasets.md) is a good place to look.
3. **Pick a design.** What comparison would convince a skeptic? See [which method to use](../econometrics/index.md#which-method-should-i-use).
4. **Start small.** Get one clean chart and one regression working, then build out.
