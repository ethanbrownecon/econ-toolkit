## Ideas for your own project

Once you have done one guided project, try one of these with the [datasets](../data/datasets.md) page:

- **Your city in numbers.** Pull your county's unemployment rate and house prices from FRED and chart them since 2000.
- **Countries over time.** Use the World Bank [API](../data/apis.md) to compare GDP per capita and life expectancy for five countries.
- **Price tracker.** [Scrape](../data/scraping.md) a practice bookshop and study how price relates to rating.
- **Replicate a chart.** Pick a chart from [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/) and rebuild it from the raw data.
